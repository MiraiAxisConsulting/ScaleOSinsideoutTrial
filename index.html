📖 ScaleOS™ Scoring Guide
How to rate Maturity, Evidence and Deployment for each capability
SCI Formula: (M × 0.50) + (E × 0.25) + (D × 0.25)
⚡ MATURITY 50%
1Not established — no process exists
2Partially established — informal or inconsistent
3Operational — defined and generally followed
4Well managed — measured and consistently applied
5World class — benchmark standard, continuously improved
📄 EVIDENCE 25%
1No evidence — nothing to show
2Document available — policy or SOP exists on paper
3Implementation verified — evidence it is being used
4Performance verified — results and outcomes evidenced
5Real-time system verified — live data confirms performance
🏢 DEPLOYMENT 25%
1Not deployed — exists nowhere in the organisation
2Pilot deployment — one team or location only
3Department deployed — multiple teams using it
4Widely deployed — most of the organisation
5Enterprise wide — entire organisation without exception
⚖️ GOVERNANCE RULES
🔒 M=1 auto-locks E=1, D=1📊 M caps max Evidence📦 E caps max Deployment🚫 NO answer locks all at 1
